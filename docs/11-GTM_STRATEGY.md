## 2. Commercial Pricing Tiers

| Tier Name | Target Customer | Price | Key Features Included |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Freemium** | Job Seekers | **$0 / mo** | 3 CV analyses/mo, 4-pillar score, basic ATS check |
| **Pro Candidate** | Active Applicants | **$19 / mo** | Unlimited analyses, GitHub/LinkedIn/Tableau/PowerBI connectors, line-by-line rewrites |
| **Enterprise Recruiter** | Hiring Teams | **$99 / mo** | Multi-seat workspace (10 seats), candidate candidate pool matrix, Executive PDF export, custom JD indexing |

## 3. Growth & Customer Acquisition Channels
1. **Product-Led Growth (PLG)**: Free scoring tool creates viral sharing of scorecard badges on LinkedIn.
2. **Developer Community Outreach**: Open-source SDK & GitHub Action for CI/CD resume optimization.
3. **Enterprise B2B Direct Sales**: Target technical staffing agencies seeking objective candidate ranking.
